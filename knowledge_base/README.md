# Scientific information sample

`rag_chunks.jsonl` contains 17 pre-existing chunks selected from Kira's curated information base: two per topic plus F018 for cached-follow-up test coverage. `sources.json` contains the 15 references linked by those chunks. These are application knowledge records and source metadata, not personal health records or full copies of scientific articles.

The sample lets judges inspect evidence metadata, citations, retrieval gating, ranking, and context construction without publishing the complete corpus or raw research files. It offers less coverage than the full application. Source IDs are retained so citations can be traced.

The selection is a technical demonstration, not an independent review of the cited evidence. The app remains a general wellness tool, and generated responses should not be treated as diagnosis or treatment advice.
